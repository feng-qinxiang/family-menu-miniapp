- vip_status
- created_at

- status

- step_text

## 2. 关键关系

- family 1:N family_member
- recipe 1:N recipe_step
- recipe 1:N recipe_ingredient
- family 1:N daily_menu
- daily_menu 1:N daily_menu_item
- family 1:N shopping_list
- shopping_list 1:N shopping_list_item
- recipe 1:N cook_history
- recipe 1:N community_post

## 3. 关键字段建议

- `source_type` 区分：自建、做过、社区、链接导入
- `audit_status` 区分：待审、通过、拒绝
- `vip_status` 直接用于权限判断
